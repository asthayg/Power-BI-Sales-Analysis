# Dashboard build guide

## Page 1: Sales Overview
Canvas: 16:9 widescreen.
Top row: four Card visuals for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Profit Margin.
Middle left: Line chart with Calendar[Month] on X-axis and [Total Sales] on Y-axis.
Middle right: Clustered bar chart with Sales[Category] and [Total Sales].
Bottom left: Clustered bar chart with Sales[Region] and [Total Sales].
Bottom right: Table with Product, Total Sales, Total Profit.
Add slicers for Calendar[Year], Sales[Region], Sales[Category].

## Page 2: Product Analysis
Bar chart: Product by Total Sales (sort descending).
Matrix: Category > Product with Total Sales and Total Profit.
Use conditional formatting on Profit.
Add a tooltip or drill-through page if you want an extra feature.

## Calendar table DAX
Calendar =
ADDCOLUMNS(
    CALENDAR(MIN(Sales[Order_Date]), MAX(Sales[Order_Date])),
    "Year", YEAR([Date]),
    "Month Number", MONTH([Date]),
    "Month", FORMAT([Date], "MMM"),
    "Year-Month", FORMAT([Date], "YYYY-MM")
)

Sort Calendar[Month] by Calendar[Month Number].
Create relationship Calendar[Date] (one) -> Sales[Order_Date] (many).
